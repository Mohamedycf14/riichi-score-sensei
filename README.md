# 🀄 Riichi Flow — The Silent Teacher of Mahjong Counting

**Riichi Flow** is not another scorekeeper. It is a **mental dojo** for players who want to *see* the hand, *feel* the waits, and *calculate* the score without ever touching a calculator. Inspired by the legacy of mahjong.horneds.com, this project reimagines the training experience as a **breathing, adaptive practice partner** — one that never judges, never rushes, and always knows the exact *han* and *fu*.

---

## 🧠 Why Another Mahjong Trainer?

Most trainers ask: *"What is the score of this hand?"*  
Riichi Flow asks: *"How did you arrive at that answer — and how fast can you do it again?"*

We built this tool for the player who has already memorized the basic yaku list but struggles with the **edge cases**: 
- The 30 *fu* vs 40 *fu* boundary when a *kan* changes everything.
- The *pinfu* tsumo that *looks* like 20 fu but *is* 20 fu.
- The *chankan* that steals a win and reshapes the entire round's economy.

This is not a cheat sheet. It is a **reflex builder** — like a metronome for your mental arithmetic.

---

## 🎯 Core Philosophy: Training, Not Testing

We avoid the word "drill" because that implies monotony. Instead, Riichi Flow uses **adaptive scenario generation**. Every session is a unique constellation of tiles, winds, and dora indicators, designed to stress-test your *specific* weaknesses.

### 🌟 Key Features That Make It Different

| Feature | What It Does | Why You'll Care |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Instant Feedback Loop** | After each answer, the correct breakdown appears (han, fu, and the *exact* yaku list) | You learn the *why*, not just the *what* |
| **Hand Speed Ladder** | Your pace is measured in *seconds per hand*, not just accuracy | Real tournament play demands speed, not just correctness |
| **Dora Blindfold Mode** | Hide the dora indicators and force yourself to calculate "minimum" and "maximum" scores | This is how pros think — prepare for the worst, hope for the best |
| **Round Persistence** | Missed hands are flagged for review in your next session | Spaced repetition, but for a very specific kind of memory |
| **Kyuushu Kyuuhai Recognizer** | Special mode for the 9-tile hand — the rarest, most nerve-wracking situation in Riichi | When it happens at the table, you'll be ready |
| **Chombo Prevention Checker** | Shows you hands that *look* valid but are actually *furiten* or *chombo* traps | Avoid the most embarrassing mistakes in the game |

## 🎨 Responsive & Multilingual by Design

Riichi Flow is built on a **mobile-first** architecture. Whether you're on a phone during a smoke break or on a desktop during a lunch break, the layout adapts fluidly. The interface scales from a 4-inch screen to a 4K monitor without losing a single tile's clarity.

We support **4 languages out of the box**:
- 🇺🇸 English (original)
- 🇯🇵 Japanese (with actual kanji terms — not just romanization)
- 🇪🇺 European Portuguese (for the growing Brazilian league)
- 🇩🇪 German (for the European Riichi scene)

Each locale includes localized *yaku names*, score *term explanations*, and even language-appropriate numeric grouping (e.g., 万 as 10,000 in Japanese context).

## 🏆 The "Flow Meter" — Your Personal Growth Graph

Imagine a progress bar that doesn't just show *"50% complete"* but instead **visualizes your reaction time curve** across 100 hands. The Flow Meter tracks:

1. **Baseline Speed** — Your average seconds-per-hand over your last 20 sessions.
2. **Variance Index** — How consistent you are. A low variance means you don't crumble under pressure.
3. **Error Heatmap** — Which *fu* values cause you the most trouble (is it always the 30/40 boundary?).

This is not vanity data. It's **actionable intelligence** that tells you exactly which chapter of the *Riichi Book 1* to re-read.
